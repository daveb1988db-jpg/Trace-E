WEB-Quarters is the headquarters for Team Spidey. It includes WEB-STER's main computer and the Spidey Vehicles. The headquarters is usually hidden underground beneath Peter's backyard behind his house, and only reveals itself when the hatch is opened or a vehicle is launched. As of the episode Stolen WEB-Quarters, the original WEB-Quarters was destroyed and sank to the bottom of the sea near the port, so Team Spidey replaced with a bigger, improvised version.

In Season 5, the Web-Spinners WEB-Quarters was given a Rescue-Webs firefighter helmet, and the garage now held the Rescue-Webs firetruck and vehicles, and was most likely remodeled off screen.

Description

As the headquarters for Team Spidey, WEB-Quarters is usually where the team spends time together when not adventuring in the city. The headquarters' exterior is shaped and made to look like Spidey's head, while the interior is mainly light blue incolour with some white webs.

Trivia

  • The headquarters being hidden underground beneath Peter's backyard is similar to the headquarters in the 2018 movie Into the Spider-Verse where the spiderheroes seek refuge.
    • However, unlike in that movie, Aunt May does not know about it.
  • TRACE-E seems to stay in both Peter's house and WEB-Quarters as sometimes she is seen in the former and other times the latter.
  • Hulk is first seen visiting WEB-Quarters in the episode The Great Green Crime Spree.
  • Black Panther is first seen visiting WEB-Quarters in the episode Rocket Rhino!
  • Ms. Marvel is first seen visiting WEB-Quarters in the episode Washed Away.
  • Tony Stark/Iron Man is first seen visiting WEB-Quarters in the episode Surprise Party Surprise.
  • CAL is the first villain who managed to infiltrate WEB-Quarters in the episode Bug in the System
  • Green Goblin is the first villain shown to know about WEB-Quarters but does not know about its location in the episode Good Guy Gobby
  • Zola is the first villain who managed to take control of WEB-Quarters in the episode Stolen WEB-Quarters
